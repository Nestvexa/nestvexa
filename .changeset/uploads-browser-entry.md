Add the framework-neutral `@nestvexa/uploads/browser` entry (`uploadFiles`,
`uploadFile`, `BrowserUploadError`, `browserUploadMessage`, `readCookie`) for
SPA pages such as Inertia React, Vue and Svelte: chunked or direct uploads with
progress, abort and server-side cleanup, returning tokens to finalize.
