# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and considerate of different backgrounds. Harassment, threats, disclosure of private information and discriminatory behavior are unacceptable. Discuss technical disagreements using reproducible evidence.

The owner must designate a private conduct reporting channel and enforcement team before opening hosted community participation. Reports should be handled confidentially, with proportionate actions ranging from guidance to removal. This original policy does not claim certification by a third-party code-of-conduct organization.
